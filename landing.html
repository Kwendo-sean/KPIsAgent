Everything finance should be.

From raw statement to boardroom-ready report — automated, accurate, and aware of how your industry actually operates.

Industry-specific KPIs

§ 03 — Method

Three steps to clarity.

From signup to insight in minutes. No setup, no manual data entry, no guesswork about which numbers actually matter.

i

Sign up & pick your industry.

Create your account, name your organization, choose your industry. KPIConsole calibrates every metric to your business type.

ii

Upload a bank statement.

Drop in a PDF or CSV. The AI extracts all transactions in the background — no templates, no mapping, no chasing missing rows.

iii

Read your dashboard.

Industry-specific KPIs, trend charts, alerts and a financial-health score. Ask the assistant any follow-up question in plain English.
